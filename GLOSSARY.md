# LandinhoBot

LandinhoBot tracks motorsport calendars and session times for people following racing categories.

## Language

**Category**:
A racing series whose calendar people can follow, such as Formula 1, Stock Car Brazil, or Turismo Nacional.
_Avoid_: Class, vehicle category

**Calendar**:
The schedule of a category's racing meetings and testing, including individual session times when available.
_Avoid_: Results, standings

**Round**:
A category's organised racing meeting, containing its individual sessions.
_Avoid_: Race when referring to the whole meeting

**Testing**:
A category's scheduled on-track activity outside its championship rounds, such as preseason testing.

**Session**:
An individual on-track activity, such as practice, qualifying, a sprint, or a race.
_Avoid_: Race when referring to all types of session

**Source**:
A publication supplying a category's calendar or session times.

**Manual correction**:
A schedule change made by a person maintaining LandinhoBot.

**Manual addition**:
A round or session entered by a person that has not been supplied by an imported source.

**Pending time**:
A session's start time that has not yet been obtained from its schedule source.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, midnight as a substitute for an unknown time

**Cancellation**:
An explicit announcement by the official source that a scheduled meeting or session will not take place.
_Avoid_: Absence from an import, pending time
